Rental Policies & Legal Notice

Legal Reminder on Vehicle Registration

The fleet consists of privately registered vehicles in Nashik. Customers are advised to verify Maharashtra rental licensing, registration category, permits, and applicable RTO regulations before operating paid rentals on public highways. BIKE RENTALS provides vehicles on an as-is basis.

1. Rental Duration & Charges

Standard daily rate is ₹400 for 24 hours. Weekly package is ₹2,800 and monthly package is ₹12,000. Additional hourly billing is ₹100/hr for delays beyond scheduled return.

2. Security Deposits

A fully refundable security deposit (default ₹500) is collected strictly at pickup in cash or UPI. Deposits are never counted as rental revenue and are refunded immediately upon safe return of the bike without damage.

3. Cancellations & Modifications

Bookings can be cancelled free of charge prior to admin approval or pickup. Once issued, rental periods are binding.

4. Customer Privacy

We collect only your full name and mobile number for booking identification and communication. No sensitive personal data or emails are stored.
